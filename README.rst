=======================
HR Hospital Management
=======================

.. ![](/static/description/icon.png)

This module automates everyday hospital operations, focusing on the relationships between doctors, interns, and patients.

Features
========
* Manage doctor profiles, categories, and intern-mentor hierarchies.
* Track patient records, calculate biological age automatically.
* Keep a detailed history of doctor-patient assignments.
* Log patient visits, diagnoses, and diseases.
* Analytical wizards for mass reassignment and report generation.

Installation
============
1. Copy the ``hr_hospital`` folder into your Odoo custom addons directory.
2. Restart your Odoo server.
3. Turn on the Developer Mode in Odoo.
4. Go to **Apps -> Update Apps List**.
5. Search for "HR Hospital" and click **Activate**.
